## What the story is about
**The Lord of the Rings** is an epic high-fantasy story about an ordinary hobbit named Frodo Baggins who must go on a dangerous quest to destroy a powerful magical artifact called the One Ring in order to save his world, Middle-earth, from the dark lord Sauron.
